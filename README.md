# Ingrain

One small thing to revise, every day.

**Live at: [ingrain-xix4.onrender.com](https://ingrain-xix4.onrender.com)**

Ingrain is a simple habit tool for keeping what you've learned fresh. You maintain a list of small things worth revisiting — a concept, a technique, a snippet of knowledge — each doable in about 15–20 minutes. Every day, Ingrain picks **one** of them for you. Things you haven't touched in a while are more likely to come up.

## How to use it

1. **Sign in with just your email** — no password. Enter your email and you'll receive a 6-digit code and a sign-in link; use either. You stay signed in until you log out.
2. **Build your list** — under *My items*, add the things you want to keep fresh. Keep each one small enough to finish in one short sitting.
3. **Show up daily** — the *Today* page gives you your one item for the day. Do it, then hit **Done**. Each item shows when you last revised it and how many times.
4. **Not feeling it?** — you get exactly **one skip per day**. Use it and the next pick is final.

That's the whole loop: add small things, trust the daily pick, press Done.
